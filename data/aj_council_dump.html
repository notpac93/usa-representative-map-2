Mayor & City Council

Overview

The Mayor and City Council aim to provide the community with fiscally responsible political leadership and to ensure that city staff provides the highest level of municipal services and quality of life assurances commensurate with the desires and resources of the community. 

Meet our Mayor and City Council Members

City Council Meetings

Meetings are scheduled to be held on the first and third Tuesdays of the month at 7 p.m. Council meetings are held in the City Council Chambers, located at 300 E. Superstition Boulevard, Apache Junction, AZ 85119. The doors to the meeting venue will be open to the public at least 15 minutes before the meeting.
