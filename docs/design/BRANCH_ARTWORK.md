## Final prompts

### legislative

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background artwork for a wide horizontal navigation card in a U.S. civic education mobile app. Create one wide panoramic 3:1 architectural illustration, full bleed, with no text, labels, typography, icons, UI, border or watermark. Style: refined architectural illustration with precise realistic civic-building details, soft stone textures, subtle painterly shading, diffused daylight, quiet pale slate sky; a coordinated monochrome silver, pale gray and cool slate palette. Match the elegant softly rendered architectural imagery already used by the app. Composition: the recognizable building dominates the right two-thirds; the left third stays calm for separately rendered navy-gradient/white-title UI. Keep the roofline, identifying architectural features and the important facade in the middle 70% of the image height so it remains recognizable when used as a wide card. Clean restrained landscaping; no crowds, politicians or invented emblems. Subject: United States Capitol in Washington, D.C., representing the legislative branch. Show the recognizable Capitol dome with Statue of Freedom and part of the columned facade, viewed from a slight low three-quarter angle. ONLY the Capitol; no White House or Supreme Court building.
